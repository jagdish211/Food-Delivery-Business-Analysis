# Final Business Insights

## 1. Customer Behavior

- Regular customers contribute a major share of total orders.
- The 31–45 age group represents an important customer segment.
- Customer types show differences in average order value.
- Customer segmentation can help improve targeted offers and retention strategies.

## 2. Revenue Pattern

- Revenue varies across different days of the week.
- High-revenue days represent important periods for the business.
- Revenue analysis can support promotional and operational planning.

## 3. Popular Restaurant Categories

- Biryani is the most popular restaurant category by order volume.
- North Indian is another high-demand category.
- Popular categories can help guide restaurant partnerships and promotions.

## 4. Payment Preference

- UPI is the most frequently used payment method.
- Credit Card is another commonly used payment option.
- Understanding payment preferences can help improve the checkout experience.

## 5. Delivery Performance

- Delivery time varies across different traffic levels.
- Higher traffic levels are associated with longer delivery times.
- Delivery-time analysis can help improve delivery planning and estimated delivery times.

## 6. Traffic & Weather Impact

- Traffic conditions have an impact on delivery operations.
- Late deliveries occur under different weather conditions.
- Traffic and weather information can be considered when planning delivery operations.

## 7. Distance vs Delivery Time

- Delivery distance shows a positive relationship with actual delivery time.
- Longer delivery distances generally require more time.
- Distance can therefore be considered when estimating delivery time.

## 8. Overall Business Insight

- Customer demand is concentrated in specific customer segments.
- Certain restaurant categories contribute strongly to order volume.
- Digital payment methods are important for customers.
- Traffic, weather and delivery distance are important operational factors.
- These insights can help improve customer targeting, restaurant partnerships,
  payment experience and delivery operations.
